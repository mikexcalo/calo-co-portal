## Square-foot rates — check the ranges

Derived by dividing the quoted total by the stated area. Worth asking whether
these hold at small and large areas, or whether there's a minimum charge.

- Hang 1/2" drywall — **$3.82/sq ft** (from $840 ÷ 220)
- Skim coat — **$12.50/sq ft** (from $375 ÷ 30 — the small area may be
  inflating this; a minimum charge would explain it)
- Texture — **$4.04/sq ft** (from $728 ÷ 180)
- Paint full wall — **$4.58/sq ft** (from $825 ÷ 180)

The skim coat figure is over three times the texture rate. That is either a
minimum charge on a small area or a genuinely different job — worth asking
rather than assuming.
