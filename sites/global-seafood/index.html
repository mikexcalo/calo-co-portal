What that looks like

Sales and marketing

Having trouble getting your product in front of the right distributor? We maintain relationships with independent foodservice distributors and corporate distributors. We also know the seafood markets in Boston and the Bronx, Philadelphia and Jessup.

Looking to place your product with a national chain? We can help you.

Sales representation

Is your product buried in a distributor warehouse? Have you done sales presentations and training with no effect?

Our team is familiar with street level work. We make calls with or without distributor reps, demonstrating your product to the people who matter, and we can act as your representative on the street.

Consulting

You run a restaurant. The season is coming and you want to add seafood. What sells and what does not? Whole fish or portion packs, fresh or frozen? How do you get waitstaff to sell it? We work through those questions with you.

Want to introduce your line to distribution without a national company taking all the profit? We can help you find an anchor and an independent distributor. Looking for private labelling? We can help you find it.

Who we work with

Pacific Empress

One of Ecuador’s oldest families in the shrimp business. Pacific Empress packs commercial lines, spec packs, retail boxes and breaded items, so you can buy the right product at a price you can afford. Farm raised Vannamei whites, and Luis and his team hold the quality.

Wide Foods

A premier company with a wide selection, carrying everything from commodity frozen fish to lobster tails, shipping from New Jersey or Florida. If you are looking for something unusual, they can find it.

Barrington Seafoods

A small northeastern company with real depth in the industry: clams, mussels, calamari and domestic Florida oysters. If you need a unique product line, they can usually source it.
